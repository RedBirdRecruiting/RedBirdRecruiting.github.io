Veterinary Surgical Oncologist

📍 Confidential Location  ·  Job ID: REDBIRD-003

Compensation Competitive + Production
Schedule Mon–Fri
Employment Full-time
Certification ACVS / ACVIM-Onc

The Opportunity

Our client, a privately-owned specialty and emergency veterinary hospital, is seeking a full-time Surgical Oncologist to expand their growing oncology program. This is a referral-driven, high-acuity practice with established surgical, anesthesia, and cardiology teams already in place, giving you the multidisciplinary support you need to deliver excellent oncologic care.

Ideal for a board-certified or residency-trained surgeon with an oncology focus who wants to build and shape a specialty service in a collaborative, privately-owned environment.

About the Practice

This boutique-level, privately-owned hospital is known for advanced specialty and emergency medicine, with a culture built around open communication, collaborative care, and compassion. Existing capabilities and services include:

  • Advanced surgical suites with laser surgery and laparoscopic / minimally invasive surgery
  • Board-certified anesthesiology and pain management, critical for oncology patients
  • Board-certified cardiology for pre-surgical cardiac clearance
  • Stem cell and regenerative medicine programs
  • Digital radiology, ultrasound, and advanced imaging
  • 24/7 emergency and critical care, your post-op patients are never alone
  • In-house animal blood bank for transfusion support

What's Offered

  • Competitive specialist compensation with production component — structure flexible
  • Monday–Friday schedule; weekends covered by the ER team
  • Opportunity to grow and shape an established surgical oncology service
  • Strong multidisciplinary team (anesthesia, cardiology, MIS already in place)
  • State-of-the-art equipment and surgical suites
  • CE allowance and support for board maintenance / specialty dues
  • Comprehensive benefits package, details provided to qualified candidates
  • Sign-on and relocation assistance available for the right candidate

What We're Looking For

  • DVM with active or eligible state veterinary license
  • Board certification (ACVS) with oncology focus, or ACVIM-Oncology, or completed residency
  • Demonstrated experience in soft tissue tumor resection and reconstructive techniques
  • Comfort with multidisciplinary case management
  • Experience with laparoscopic / minimally invasive oncologic surgery a plus
  • Excellent communicator, with referring veterinarians and clients facing difficult diagnoses
  • Collaborative, team-oriented mindset; interest in service-line growth
