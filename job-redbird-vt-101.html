About the Practice

This is a well-established, AAHA-accredited companion animal hospital in Burlington, Vermont — a college town with a strong outdoor culture, affordable cost of living, and a genuinely loyal client base. The team is 4 DVMs strong with a support staff ratio that actually lets you practice medicine. The hospital is privately owned and has no corporate affiliation.

The Role

They're looking for an associate DVM to join a collaborative, low-drama team. The caseload is primarily dogs and cats with a mix of wellness, sick, and soft-tissue surgery. New grads are welcome — there's an experienced medical director on-site who actively mentors and is available for case consults.

What's on the Table

  • Base salary $130K–$165K depending on experience
  • Production bonus on top of base
  • 4 × 10-hour shifts, no overnight or emergency call
  • Health, dental, and vision insurance
  • Paid CE allowance + dedicated CE days
  • AVMA PLIT professional liability coverage
  • VIN membership
  • State licensure and DEA reimbursement
  • Relocation assistance available

What They're Looking For

  • DVM or VMD from an accredited program (new grads welcome)
  • Active Vermont veterinary license or ability to obtain one
  • Solid small animal medicine fundamentals
  • A collaborative, client-first communication style
  • Comfort with routine soft-tissue surgery preferred but not required

About Burlington, VT

Burlington sits on the eastern shore of Lake Champlain with the Green Mountains as a backdrop. It's a small city with outsized livability — great food and coffee scene, world-class skiing and hiking within 30 minutes, and the University of Vermont bringing energy to the community year-round. Cost of living is reasonable relative to other Northeast markets.
