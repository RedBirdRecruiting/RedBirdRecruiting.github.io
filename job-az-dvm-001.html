GP / Urgent Care Veterinarian (DVM)

📍 Phoenix Metro, AZ  ·  Multi-Hospital Network  ·  Job ID: AZ-DVM-001

Compensation $150K-$200K+ ProSal
Schedule 3x12s or 4x10s, Rotating Weekends
Employment Full-time
Experience 2+ Years Preferred

The Opportunity

Our client is a growing multi-hospital veterinary network in the Phoenix metro area seeking a full-time GP/Urgent Care Veterinarian to join their collaborative team. This is a growth position created to support a thriving practice focused on general practice, preventive care, urgent care, dentistry, and surgery - no specialty referral procedures required.

Whether you're looking for schedule flexibility, strong technician support, or a culture genuinely committed to reducing burnout, this is a modern, well-supported environment where each doctor helps build their own path. Leadership opportunities are also available for those interested in a Medical Director role.

About the Practice

This is an established, growing network of hospitals across the Phoenix metro, each equipped with modern technology and strong support staff. The practice is fully paperless, runs a leading cloud-based practice management system, and maintains a healthy doctor-to-tech ratio with multiple assistants and technicians assigned daily. Relief support is utilized to cover gaps.

Average caseload runs roughly 15-25 appointments per day depending on location and shift. There are no on-call or after-hours expectations.

What's Offered

  • Competitive ProSal compensation, $150,000-$200,000+ depending on experience, schedule, and production plan - paid quarterly, with each doctor building their own targets
  • Flexible scheduling (3x12s or 4x10s, 10-12 hour shifts) with shared weekend rotation
  • Medical (PPO and HSA), Dental, Vision, and Company-Paid Life Insurance
  • Short-Term and Long-Term Disability, Accident, Critical Illness, and Hospital Indemnity coverage
  • PTO, Arizona Paid Sick Time, and Holiday Pay (or swap for PTO)
  • CE allowance, DEA and State License reimbursement, and Professional Liability Insurance (PLIT)
  • Employee Pet Care Program and Pet Discounts, plus Scrub/Uniform Allowance
  • Sign-on bonus, relocation assistance, and CE allowance available - negotiable
  • Production opportunities and leadership pathways
  • A collaborative culture with leadership committed to professional development and work-life balance

What We're Looking For

  • Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM or equivalent)
  • Active Arizona veterinary license
  • At least 2 years of experience preferred
  • Strong medical and surgical skills, plus dentistry experience
  • Excellent client communication
  • Team-oriented, adaptable, positive, and patient-focused
  • Willingness and ability to work closing shifts (8pm)
  • Nice-to-have: urgent care, ultrasound, endoscopy, CT, exotics, or leadership experience

About Phoenix

The Phoenix metro offers year-round sunshine, a fast-growing economy, and an affordable lifestyle relative to other major metros. From the hiking trails of Camelback Mountain and the South Mountain preserve to the dining, arts, and sports scenes downtown, there's something for every interest. The Valley's communities blend suburban comfort with easy access to Sedona, Flagstaff, and the high country for weekend escapes.
