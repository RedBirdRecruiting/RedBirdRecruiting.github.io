The Opportunity

Our client, a privately-owned specialty and emergency veterinary hospital, is seeking a full-time Emergency Department Veterinarian to join their 24/7 ER team. This is a high-acuity, referral-rich practice with strong on-site specialty backup, including board-certified surgery, cardiology, and anesthesiology, so you'll never be working a critical case alone.

Whether you're an experienced ER vet, ECC residency-trained, or an experienced GP ready to transition into emergency medicine, this is a chance to practice high-quality emergency care in a supportive, well-resourced environment.

About the Practice

This is a boutique-level, privately-owned hospital focused on advanced specialty and emergency care. The culture emphasizes open communication, collaborative medicine, and compassion, both for patients and team members. Capabilities and services include:

  • 24/7 emergency and critical care
  • Advanced surgery, orthopedic, soft tissue, surgical oncology, minimally invasive
  • Board-certified cardiology and anesthesiology
  • Stem cell and regenerative medicine
  • In-house animal blood bank
  • Digital radiology, ultrasound, and advanced diagnostic imaging

What's Offered

  • Competitive compensation commensurate with experience
  • Strong specialty backup (surgery, cardiology, anesthesiology on team)
  • State-of-the-art diagnostics and surgical capabilities
  • Collaborative, supportive culture with experienced ER team
  • Opportunities for professional growth and continuing education
  • Comprehensive benefits package, details provided to qualified candidates

What We're Looking For

  • Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM or equivalent)
  • Active or eligible state veterinary license
  • Comfort managing high-acuity emergency cases independently
  • Strong triage, stabilization, and critical care skills
  • Experience with toxicities, trauma, and emergency stabilization surgery
  • ECC certification, residency, or internship a plus, but not required
  • Strong communicator; calm under pressure; team-oriented
Confidential search. Practice name and location details are shared with qualified candidates after an initial conversation. Redbird will never contact your current employer without your explicit permission.
