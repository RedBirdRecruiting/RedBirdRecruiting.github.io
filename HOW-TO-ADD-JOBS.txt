REDBIRD RECRUITING — HOW TO ADD A NEW JOB
==========================================

Every new job requires two things:
  1. A new job detail PAGE  (e.g. job-redbird-xx-000.html)
  2. A new job CARD added to jobs.html

Formspree automatically receives the job ID and title with every
application because of two hidden fields in the apply form.
You will see them labeled "job_id" and "job_title" in every
Formspree submission email.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 1 — PICK A JOB ID AND FILE NAME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use this format:
  Job ID:    REDBIRD-XX-000   (XX = 2-letter state, 000 = next number)
  File name: job-redbird-xx-000.html

Examples already in use:
  REDBIRD-OH-001  →  job-redbird-oh-001.html
  REDBIRD-VT-101  →  job-redbird-vt-101.html

Your next job would be something like:
  REDBIRD-TX-002  →  job-redbird-tx-002.html


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 2 — CREATE THE JOB DETAIL PAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Copy job-redbird-oh-001.html and rename it (e.g. job-redbird-tx-002.html).

Then find and update these 9 things inside the file:

  1. <title> tag at the top
       Change to: Job Title — Location | Redbird Recruiting

  2. <meta name="description"> tag
       Write a 1-sentence summary of the role.

  3. The pill badges (role / type / region labels)
       <span>Veterinarian</span><span>Full-time</span><span>Midwest</span>

  4. The <h1> job title

  5. The location + Job ID line
       📍 City, State  ·  Job ID: REDBIRD-XX-000

  6. The four meta boxes (Compensation, Schedule, Employment, Experience)

  7. The job description sections
       (The Opportunity, About the Practice, What's Offered,
        What We're Looking For, and any location section)

  8. The two hidden fields in the apply form — MOST IMPORTANT FOR FORMSPREE:
       <input type="hidden" name="job_id"    value="REDBIRD-XX-000" />
       <input type="hidden" name="job_title" value="Full Job Title — Location" />

  9. The species dropdown (optional — remove or change options to suit the role)


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 3 — ADD A CARD TO jobs.html
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Open jobs.html and paste the card template below inside the
<div class="js-list" id="job-list"> block, after the last </div>.

Fill in the 6 marked fields and make sure data-role / data-type /
data-region match the allowed values listed at the bottom of this file.

── CARD TEMPLATE (copy everything between the lines) ──────────

          <div class="js-item" data-role="ROLE" data-type="TYPE" data-region="REGION">
            <div>
              <h3>JOB TITLE</h3>
              <div class="js-meta">
                <span>City, State</span>
                <span>Full-time</span>
                <span>$000K–$000K</span>
              </div>
              <div class="js-pills">
                <span>Tag</span><span>Tag</span><span>Tag</span>
              </div>
            </div>
            <a class="btn btn-primary btn-sm" href="job-redbird-xx-000.html">Apply</a>
          </div>

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ALLOWED VALUES FOR DATA ATTRIBUTES
  data-role   →  dvm | tech | leadership | manager
  data-type   →  full-time | part-time | relief
  data-region →  northeast | southeast | midwest | southwest | west

These must match exactly (lowercase) or the sidebar filters won't work.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOW FORMSPREE IDENTIFIES EACH APPLICATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every job detail page has these two hidden fields inside the form:

  <input type="hidden" name="job_id"    value="REDBIRD-OH-001" />
  <input type="hidden" name="job_title" value="Full-Time Veterinarian (DVM)..." />

When someone submits an application, Formspree sends you an email
that includes ALL form fields — including these hidden ones.
Your inbox will show something like:

  job_id:    REDBIRD-OH-001
  job_title: Full-Time Veterinarian (DVM), Mixed-Animal Practice — Eastern Ohio
  name:      Jane Smith
  email:     jane@email.com
  phone:     555-000-0000
  ...

This means you always know exactly which role each applicant
is responding to, with no extra setup required in Formspree.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
REMOVING A FILLED ROLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Delete (or keep archived) the job-redbird-xx-000.html file.
2. In jobs.html, delete the entire <div class="js-item"> block
   for that role — from the opening <div> to its closing </div>.

You do NOT need to touch styles.css or main.js when adding
or removing jobs.
