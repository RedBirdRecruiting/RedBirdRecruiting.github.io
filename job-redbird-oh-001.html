Full-Time Veterinarian (DVM)

The Opportunity

Our client is a well-established rural mixed-animal practice in eastern Ohio seeking a full-time Veterinarian to join their collaborative team. In operation for over a decade, this practice serves a tri-state region (OH, PA, WV) and has built a strong reputation for compassionate, high-quality care across species.

Whether you're a new graduate eager to build broad hands-on experience or a seasoned DVM looking for a supportive, team-oriented environment in a welcoming rural community, this could be the right fit.

About the Practice

This is a true mixed-animal practice offering a wide range of services across species:

  • Small animal — wellness exams, dental care, surgery, and diagnostics
  • Equine — lameness, reproduction, dentistry, ultrasound, and digital radiology
  • Large animal / food animal — herd checks, vaccinations, reproductive services, dairy work, and small ruminants
  • Mobile services — farm calls throughout the region alongside in-clinic appointments

The team includes multiple DVMs with diverse specialties — equine lameness and reproduction, acupuncture and chiropractic, dairy and small ruminants — alongside Registered Veterinary Technicians, veterinary assistants, and experienced support staff. You'll have mentorship, genuine collaboration, and caseload variety that most practices simply can't offer.

What's Offered

  • Competitive salary $120,000–$160,000 based on experience, with PROSAL
  • Supportive, collaborative team environment
  • Mentorship program for new graduates
  • Wide variety of cases across small animal, equine, and large animal
  • Modern diagnostic tools including digital radiology and ultrasound
  • Monday–Friday schedule — clinic closed on weekends
  • Opportunity to develop special interests and pursue certifications
  • Beautiful rural setting with a tight-knit, appreciative client base

What We're Looking For

  • Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM or equivalent)
  • Active or eligible Ohio veterinary license (PA and WV licensure a plus)
  • Comfort or genuine interest in mixed-animal practice — willingness to learn across species
  • Strong communication skills and genuine care for clients and patients
  • Team-oriented attitude
  • New graduates warmly welcomed — full mentorship provided

About Eastern Ohio

Eastern Ohio offers an affordable, community-centered lifestyle with access to the natural beauty of Appalachian foothills, rolling farmland, and the Cuyahoga Valley. The tri-state border region is a tight-knit agricultural community where veterinarians are genuinely valued members of the local fabric. Pittsburgh and Columbus are both within driving distance for weekend getaways.
