Veterinary Cardiologist

📍 Confidential Location  ·  Job ID: REDBIRD-001

Compensation Competitive Specialist
Schedule Mon–Fri
Employment Full-time or Part-time
Certification ACVIM (Cardiology)

The Opportunity

Our client, a privately-owned specialty and emergency veterinary hospital, is seeking a board-certified or residency-trained Veterinary Cardiologist to join their specialty team. With high-acuity surgical and emergency caseloads on-site, your cardiology expertise will be central to pre-surgical cardiac clearance, complex case management, and referral cardiology consults.

Schedule is flexible, full-time or part-time arrangements available. Ideal for a cardiologist seeking a collaborative, privately-owned hospital with strong specialty support.

About the Practice

A boutique-level, privately-owned specialty and emergency hospital known for compassionate, collaborative medicine. The cardiology service partners closely with surgery, anesthesia, and the 24/7 ER team. Capabilities include:

  • Advanced echocardiography and cardiac diagnostics
  • Board-certified surgery and anesthesiology, close pre-op cardiac collaboration
  • Advanced orthopedic, soft tissue, and minimally invasive surgical programs
  • 24/7 ER and critical care for inpatient cardiac cases
  • Stem cell and regenerative medicine offerings
  • Digital radiology, ultrasound, and advanced imaging
  • In-house blood bank for transfusion support

What's Offered

  • Competitive specialist compensation
  • Monday–Friday specialty hours, ER team handles weekends and after-hours
  • Strong referral base from local primary care veterinarians
  • Multidisciplinary team (surgery, anesthesia, and ER on-site)
  • CE allowance and board/dues support
  • Comprehensive benefits package, details provided to qualified candidates
  • Relocation assistance available for the right candidate

What We're Looking For

  • DVM with active or eligible state veterinary license
  • ACVIM (Cardiology) board certification, or completed residency
  • Strong echocardiography skills (Doppler, advanced imaging)
  • Comfort supporting surgical and anesthesia teams with cardiac risk assessment
  • Interventional cardiology experience (PDA, balloon valvuloplasty, pacemaker) a plus
  • Excellent client communicator, cardiac diagnoses can be emotionally heavy
  • Strong collaborator with referring veterinarians and on-site specialty teams
Confidential search. Practice name and location details are shared with qualified candidates after an initial conversation. Redbird will never contact your current employer without your explicit permission.
