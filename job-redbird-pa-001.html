Associate Veterinarian (DVM)

📍 Greater Pittsburgh, PA  ·  Job ID: REDBIRD-PA-001

Compensation $130K-$170K + ProSal (22%)
Schedule Mon-Fri, No Weekends, No On-Call
Employment Full-time or Part-time
Experience 3-7 Years Preferred

The Opportunity

Our client is a well-established small animal general practice in the greater Pittsburgh area seeking an experienced Associate Veterinarian. This is a busy, well-supported practice with a genuinely collaborative culture and remarkably low turnover across both the doctor and support staff.

This role is open to full-time or part-time candidates. It's a strong fit for a DVM who's confident seeing a full appointment schedule and performing routine surgery, enjoys a fast-paced flow, and wants a predictable weekday schedule with no on-call, no after-hours, and no Saturday appointments.

About the Practice

This is a small animal general practice offering wellness, surgery, dental, and in-house diagnostics. The team is built for efficiency and support:

  • Three doctors on staff (two full-time, one part-time)
  • Nine veterinary technicians and two client-facing receptionists
  • Two techs per doctor on appointment days, two surgical techs per surgeon on procedure days
  • Four exam rooms, one surgical suite, one dental unit, and a dedicated wet table
  • Avimark with fully electronic medical records

The culture here is a real selling point. The current doctors have been with the practice for ten, six, and four years respectively, and several support staff members have been on the team for a decade or more. It's a supportive group that gets along and backs each other up.

Schedule and Caseload

  • Full-time: Monday-Friday with one weekday off
  • Part-time: two to three days per week
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 9am-6pm, Fri 9am-5pm
  • No Saturday appointments, no on-call, no after-hours
  • Roughly 15 appointments per day, plus two to three same-day sick slots
  • Five to eight surgeries depending on the schedule and doctor skill set
  • Note: true emergency cases are not handled at this practice

What's Offered

  • Competitive salary $130,000-$170,000 with ProSal (22% production)
  • Sign-on bonus
  • Relocation assistance negotiable
  • CE allowance
  • Employer-sponsored health insurance with percentage of premium covered (dental and vision optional)
  • 401(k) plus year-end profit-share distribution
  • PTO days and paid holidays
  • PLIT and license reimbursement
  • Employee pet discount
  • Ownership path available

What We're Looking For

  • Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM or equivalent)
  • Active or eligible Pennsylvania veterinary license
  • 3-7 years of experience preferred
  • Willing and able to both see appointments and perform surgery
  • Strong time management and multitasking skills in a fast-paced setting
  • Someone who can work independently but collaborates well with the team

About the Pittsburgh Area

Greater Pittsburgh offers an affordable cost of living paired with the amenities of a real city. Candidates get walkable neighborhoods, a strong food and arts scene, three rivers and plenty of green space, and easy access to the surrounding countryside for weekend getaways. It's a welcoming, community-minded region where a good vet quickly becomes a valued part of the neighborhood.
