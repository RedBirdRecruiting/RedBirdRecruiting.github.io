Engagement Models

Flexible ways to work together.

Contingent Search

You pay only if we place a candidate you hire. Ideal for most open roles.

  • No upfront cost
  • Retention guarantee included
  • Fast time-to-shortlist

Retained Search

A focused, priority search for hard-to-fill or senior roles, with a dedicated lead.

  • Structured milestones
  • Exclusive partnership
  • Confidential sourcing

Practice-Group Partnership

  • Preferred pricing
  • Named account lead
  • Quarterly hiring reviews
What You Get

A partner who does the work, not a résumé forwarder.

A real intake

We spend real time with you up-front. What kind of medicine? What does a great day look like? What's the comp structure? That context shapes every outreach we make.

Active sourcing

We reach passive candidates — clinicians who aren't actively applying but would consider the right role. That's most of the best hires.

Honest screening

We evaluate candidates on medicine, lifestyle, culture, and long-term fit — and we tell you the truth about each one.

Offer & onboarding support

We help you structure competitive offers, coach the close, and stay involved through onboarding so hires actually stick.
