About Us

We built Redbird because veterinary hiring deserved better.

We're a small, veterinary-only recruiting and practice advisory team. We match practices and people who will both be glad, twelve months in, that we introduced them, and we help practice owners see exactly where their hospitals are leaving money.

Our story

Hiring that understands the exam room.

Redbird Recruiting was started by people who've spent real time inside veterinary medicine — working with practice owners, watching great techs burn out from bad fits, and seeing how hard it is for hospitals to find the right clinician in a crowded market.

Generic recruiting firms treat a DVM search like any other role. We don't. Every candidate conversation starts with medicine: caseload, surgery appetite, emergency comfort, mentorship style, and the day-to-day reality of the practice.

What we value

  • Honesty, even when it costs us a placement.
  • Depth over volume. Fewer, better candidates.
  • Respect for the clinicians we represent.
  • Long-term retention, not short-term close rates.
Meet Jason Redbird

He learned the business side long before veterinary medicine.

As an area manager for a national company, Jason ran 11 locations doing more than $12 million a year, with more than 70 employees and eight branch managers reporting to him, and used strategic planning and Six Sigma to take every location from losses to profit at a 20% overall margin. That experience translates directly to practice owners with more than one location, who face the same test of consistent standards, accountable managers and every site pulling its weight.

He brought that same approach into veterinary medicine, where as a practice manager for a national veterinary group he ran a five-doctor hospital, led turnarounds at over a dozen hospitals and took one team from among the lowest engagement scores in the company to among the best in eight months, without losing a single person. Later, building talent systems for a multi-state practice group, he managed pipelines of 300 to 400 candidates, doubled recruiting throughput and trained hiring managers, so when a practice's plan calls for a doctor, Redbird can run that search as part of the work.

Today he puts all of it into one plan. Redbird's Practice Performance toolkit scores 33 KPIs against industry benchmarks, prices each gap in dollars and shows EBITDA under four scenarios, focusing on the levers that move margin, from doctor capacity and no-shows to fees, supply costs and payroll. Because engaged teams cost less and produce more, every plan also prices in turnover, compares each role's pay with the local market and includes an anonymous staff survey. Behind it is the discipline Jason built in the Navy, where he managed more than $150 million in aviation equipment and earned four promotions in three years.
