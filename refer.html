What counts as a qualified referral

  • They hold an active DVM (or equivalent) credential.
  • They're hired by a Redbird client into a full-time or eligible part-time role.
  • They haven't applied to or spoken with Redbird in the last 12 months.
  • You submitted them before our team contacted them.
  • They confirm you as the referrer during the hiring process.

How it works

  • Submit the form with whatever contact info you have — even a name and rough role is enough.
  • We reach out to your colleague privately. No pressure, no hard sell.
  • If there's a fit, we match them to the right opportunity.
  • Once they're placed and hit the milestones, we pay you.
